Our story

It begins at the shoreline.

Every piece starts with a colour from the water: deep blue, turquoise, sea foam, wet sand. We build the collection around those shades, so a pearl, a crystal and a cord all look like they belong to the same tide.

Then we choose what glows.

Faux pearls, moonstone-look beads, rose quartz and crystal accents, picked for a soft lustre that sits well against skin. Dressed up for a wedding or worn with a tee, they hold their own.

Hands finish the rest.

Strung, knotted and woven by hand, then sent to you with love. If the piece you imagine does not exist yet, we make custom orders, down to your colours and wrist size.
